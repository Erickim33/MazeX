MazeX Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

MazeX ("the app") is a maze game developed by Eric Kimani. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use MazeX.

Information We Collect

MazeX does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, or other personal information to play the game.

The app may process technical information associated with advertising, such as device or advertising identifiers, approximate location, IP address, and information about ad interactions. This information may be processed by our advertising provider, Google AdMob, to provide and measure advertisements, prevent fraud, and support advertising services.

Advertising

MazeX uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including interstitial and rewarded advertisements.

Google may collect and process information from your device in connection with advertising. Google's use of information is governed by Google's own privacy policies and terms.

For more information about how Google handles data, please visit Google's Privacy Policy .

Children's Privacy

MazeX is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information associated with the app. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Third-Party Services

MazeX uses third-party services, including Google AdMob, which may independently process information according to their own policies.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or MazeX, you can contact the developer through the contact information provided on the MazeX Google Play listing.
